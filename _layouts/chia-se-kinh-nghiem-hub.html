{%- comment -%} THE H1 NAMES THE PLACE; "BÀI VIẾT MỚI NHẤT" STILL LABELS THE LIST. History, because this reverses half of a decision and keeps the other half. The hub originally hid its h1 and let the title-bar lockup speak for the page. On 2026-07-15 the owner replaced that with a plain, honest label for the list below it — "Bài viết mới nhất" — as the h1. The instinct was right and is kept: the reader arriving at a list should be met by a label for that list, not by a slogan. What was wrong is that the label was doing it as the H1. This is the highest-authority page of the whole library, the one page trying to rank for "chia sẻ kinh nghiệm … xưởng nhuộm", and its single strongest on-page signal said "Latest posts" — a phrase that describes any feed on the internet and names neither the subject nor the place. It also left the page with no prose at all: an h1, a list of cards, a rail. Nothing told a first-time visitor arriving cold from a search result what they had found or why the person writing it would know. So the two jobs are split, which costs nothing and settles both: h1 — what this place is lead — who writes it and on what authority, in one line h2 — "Bài viết mới nhất", still directly above the list it labels "xưởng nhuộm" rather than "nhà máy dệt nhuộm" in the h1 because it is what people in the trade actually say, and it is shorter. {%- endcomment -%}
