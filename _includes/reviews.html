{%- comment -%} THE REVIEW MICRODATA THAT WAS IN THIS FILE HAS MOVED. It was not deleted: it is in meta.html now, as JSON-LD on the Alpha Smart Dyehouse SoftwareApplication. Why it had to move. These `itemprop`s hung off the element in page.html, which is `itemscope itemtype="schema.org/Organization"`. That made each of them a review OF ALPHA THE COMPANY, and Google's review-snippet policy permanently excludes reviews of the publishing entity as self-serving. The markup was doing nothing and could never do anything. An earlier note here argued that was nonetheless the honest home, on the reading that the reviewers were describing working with Alpha rather than rating a product. That reading was wrong, and the owner corrected it on 2026-08-10: both reviews are about Alpha Smart Dyehouse. The text agrees — the first names the Spectro machines, the dispensers, the dye-machine controllers, the QR flow, and an RFT move from 80% to 90%, which are this product's features and this product's outcome. Attached to a product, first-party reviews ARE eligible for the rich result. Keeping these attributes while the JSON-LD claims the same two reviews for the product would declare two different subjects for one review, so they came out rather than being duplicated. WHAT MUST STAY TRUE. The JSON-LD quotes `t.review_*_body` through `strip_html`, so the structured data cannot drift from the words below. But Google requires reviewed content to be VISIBLE on the page: if this section is ever removed, the `review` array in meta.html goes with it. EVERY `itemprop` IN THE CARDS WENT, NOT JUST THE FOUR ON THE WRAPPERS. `reviewBody`, `author`, `jobTitle`, `worksFor` and the portraits' `image` were all properties of the Review scope. With that scope gone they would not have become inert — an orphaned `itemprop` attaches to the nearest enclosing `itemscope`, which here is the element. The page would have ended up asserting `Organization.reviewBody`, `Organization.author` and, worst of the three, `Organization.image` pointing at a photograph of a customer. Every one of those facts is now stated correctly in the JSON-LD instead. {%- endcomment -%}
