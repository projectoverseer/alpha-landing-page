Alpha Software {%- comment -%} THE BAR CARRIES WHAT THE PAGE CANNOT DO FOR ITSELF. This is one long page. It can scroll to its own sections — a reader does that with a thumb, and does it without being told. What it cannot do on its own is open a specific product panel, cross to the reading hub, dial us, or change language. Those four are chrome; a section anchor is not. On the expanded bar the anchors needed ~648px against ~565px of free width and the labels wrapped to a second line — measured on the live build, not guessed. CORRECTION, 2026-07-28: the previous pass hid them with a `nav-item--toc` class above lg, on the stated reasoning that below lg they lived in a drawer. There is no drawer. This navbar has no toggler — `.navbar-collapse` is plain `.collapse`, so under lg it is `display: none` and the entire list is already invisible there. Hiding them above lg hid them from the only width that ever showed them. So they are `visually-hidden` + `tabindex="-1"` now, which is exactly what the three anchors below and above this block already were, and what they should have been from the start: present in the DOM and in the accessibility tree, out of the tab order (so no keyboard user lands on a link they cannot see — SC 2.4.7), and still there for scrollspy, which needs a link per observed section to track the whole page. KNOWN AND NOT FIXED HERE: because there is no drawer, `Các sản phẩm` and the hub link are invisible below 1024px too — that is, to four out of five readers. That is a bigger change than this pass, and it is flagged rather than quietly patched. {%- endcomment -%}
